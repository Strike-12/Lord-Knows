BACKGROUND & ASPIRATIONS

About Elsen

I'm a student at Grossmont High School. My main passions are clothing, football, business, and reselling streetwear and sneakers. Outside of school, I spend a lot of time drawing anime, playing football, and reselling clothes.

In my web development class, we've been learning how to code websites, work with GitHub, and build projects. I took what I learned and reached out to a real company offering to build their website. They hired me, I built the site, and I made $200 on my first client project.

When I'm not in class or on the field, I'm drawing anime characters in my sketchbook, gaming, hanging out with friends, and sourcing clothes to resell. I'm focused on building my own streetwear brand, Lord Knows, and growing my clothing business.
